*Draft in progress. Sections are written once discovery settles them; see `.memlog.md` for decisions so far.*

## Who This Serves

**Primary: the Director of Water and Sanitation.** Accountable for the metro's open water and
sewer requests, with a finite pool of field crews spread across depots. Once a week they decide
where those crews go next.

[ASSUMPTION — confirm with a practitioner] Today that decision leans on backlog totals by
department and on whichever complaints escalate through councillors and the media. That favours
the loudest areas over the oldest and most severe backlog.

[ASSUMPTION — proposed success signal] In under five minutes on a Monday, the Director can see
where open work is concentrated, how old it is, and whether last week's reallocation reduced it.

**Not served in the first release:** residents, ward councillors, call-centre agents.
